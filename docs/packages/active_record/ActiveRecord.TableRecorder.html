    Part of Active Record

    Summary

    Methods

    init(recorder, table_name)
    Creates the object that change_table hands to its callback while statements are being recorded. It has the column type methods of a real table definition, as well as remove, rename, timestamps and references, and records each call as a statement.
    references(name, ...)
    Records the addition of a reference column.
    remove(name)
    Records the removal of a column.
    rename(name, new_name)
    Records the renaming of a column.
    timestamps(options)
    Records the addition of the timestamp columns.
