    PaintButton(panel, w, h)
    Draws an fl_button: the outline, background, title and FontAwesome icon, according to the settings of the button.
    PaintButton(panel, w, h)
    Draws a Derma button with a border and a fill that reflects its disabled, pressed or hovered state, and sets its text color.
    PaintCharCreationBasePanel(panel, w, h)
    Draws the title of a character creation stage, if the panel has one.
    PaintCharCreationLoadPanel(panel, w, h)

    Draws the title of the character creation screen.

    Draws the name of the character on a character card and outlines the card if it belongs to the active character of the local player.
    PaintCollapsibleCategory(panel, w, h)
    Draws the header background of a collapsible category, darker while it is collapsed, and applies the theme font to its header.
    PaintConfigLine(panel, w, h)
    Draws a darker background on the lines of the config editor that are marked as dark.
    PaintDeathScreen(cur_time, scrw, scrh)
    Draws the death screen with the respawn countdown and progress bar, and sets up the white fade for the last 3 seconds before the respawn.
    PaintFrame(panel, w, h)
    Draws the background and the title of an fl_frame.

    Draws the translucent background of a frame and its header gradient in the accent color.
    PaintInventoryBackground(panel, w, h)
    Draws the translucent backdrop around an inventory panel.
    PaintItemSlot(panel, w, h)
    Draws the gradient background of an inventory item slot.
    PaintListView(panel, w, h)
    Draws a white background for a list view if its background is enabled.
    PaintListViewLine(panel)
    Draws a line of a list view, colored after its selected, hovered or alternate state, and sets the text color of its columns.
    PaintMainMenu(panel, width, height)
    Draws the background of the main menu and its top bar with the schema's logo (or name), description and author.
    PaintMenu(panel, w, h)
    Draws the dark background of a Derma menu.
    PaintMenuOption(panel, w, h)
    Draws the highlight of a hovered menu option and sets its text color accordingly.
    PaintOverInventoryBackground(panel, w, h)
    Draws the title of an inventory above its panel, if it has one.
    PaintOverMenu(panel)
    Does nothing: the skin draws nothing over Derma menus.
    PaintPermissionButton(perm_panel, btn, w, h)
    Draws a button of the permission editor, colored and labeled after its permission value, with a selection box and a clock icon for temporary permissions.
    PaintScoreboard(panel, width, height)
    Draws the background, title and column headers of the scoreboard.
    PaintScrollBarGrip(panel)
    Draws the grip of a scroll bar as a black box with a white border.
    PaintSidebar(panel, width, height)
    Draws the background of an fl_sidebar.
    PaintTab(panel, w, h)
    Draws a tab of a property sheet, highlighted if it is the active one.
    PaintTabInventoryBackground(panel, w, h)
    Draws the frame, the gradients and the character name around the player model of the inventory tab. Does nothing if the panel has no player model.
    PaintTabMenu(panel, width, height)
    Blurs the screen behind the tab menu, easing the blur size toward the blur target of the menu, and draws an indicator bar for the button stored in the menu's activeBtn field.
    PaintTabMenuButtonPanel(panel, width, height)
    Draws the translucent background of the button bar of the tab menu.
    SchemeForm(panel)
    Applies the font, upper case text, color and shadow of the skin to the label of a form.
    SchemeListViewLabel(panel)
    Sets the text inset and the text color of a list view label.
    SchemeMenuOption(panel)
    Sets the text color of a menu option.
