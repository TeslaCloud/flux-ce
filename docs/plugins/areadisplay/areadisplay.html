    Methods

    HUDPaint()
    Draws the queued text area notices at the left side of the screen and drops the ones that have expired.
    InitPostEntity()
    Currently does nothing; loading of the saved areas is commented out.
    load()
    Loads the areas saved in the 'areas' plugin data and registers each of them. Serverside only.
    PlayerEnteredTextArea(actor, area, cur_time)
    Queues a notice to be displayed on the HUD for 8 seconds. The displayed text is currently a hardcoded placeholder.
    PlayerInitialized(actor)
    Currently does nothing; sending the text areas to the player is commented out.
    save()
    Currently does nothing; saving of the text areas is commented out.
    SaveData()
    Currently does nothing; saving of the areas is commented out.
