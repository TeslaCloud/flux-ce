    AddTabMenuItems(menu)client
    Adds the inventory to the tab menu.
    CanItemMenuOpen(item_obj)client

    CanItemMove(item_obj, inventory, x, y)server
    Checks whether the item can be moved inside of the inventory: asks the can_move callback of the item and prevents moving items in disabled inventories.
    CanItemTransfer(item_obj, inventory, x, y)server
    Checks whether the item can be transferred to the inventory: equipment and pockets restrictions, the can_transfer callback of the item, the can_contain callback of the container the inventory belongs to, and whether the inventory is disabled.
    CreatePlayerInventories(owner, inventories)server
    Creates the default set of the player's inventories: main inventory, hotbar, pockets and the equipment slots.

    Calculates the size of the pockets inventory based on the items in it.
    ItemTransferred(item_obj, new_inventory, old_inventory)server
    Closes the inventory of a transferred container item for the players who were viewing it and do not have the container anymore.
    OnActiveCharacterSet(owner, character)server
    Recreates inventories of the player when their active character changes.
    OnContextMenuClose()client
    Removes the hotbar when the context menu closes.
    OnContextMenuOpen()client
    Shows the hotbar and makes it interactive while the context menu is open.
    OnInventoryRebuild(panel)client
    Keeps the pockets panel no wider than the main inventory panel.

    Marks the item instance that has just been created as not rotated.

    Tells the client to rebuild the player model preview when a wearable item is equipped.
    OnItemUnequipped(owner, item_obj)server
    Tells the client to rebuild the player model preview when a wearable item is unequipped.
    OnMenuPanelOpen(menu_panel, active_panel)client
    Closes the windows of all opened container items when a tab menu panel opens.
    PlayerBindPress(client, bind, pressed)shared
    Calls the 'PlayerSelectSlot' plugin hook when the player presses one of the slot binds.
    PlayerDisconnected(actor)server
    Deletes inventories of the disconnected player from the server cache.
    PlayerDropItem(actor, instance_ids)server
    Takes the items out of their inventory and spawns them in front of the player.
    PlayerSelectSlot(client, slot)shared
    Selects the weapon of the item in the specified hotbar slot, or uses the item if it is not an equipable one.
    PlayerTakeItem(actor, item_obj, ...)server
    Puts an item lying in the world into one of the player's inventories and removes its entity.
    PlayerThrewGrenade(actor, entity)server
    Takes the equipped throwable items away from the player who has thrown a grenade.
    PlayerUsedItem(actor, item_obj, act, ...)server
    Synchronizes the inventory of an item after a menu action has been performed on it.
    PlayerUseItem(actor, item_obj, ...)server
    Calls the on_use callback of the item, then takes the item out of its inventory (or removes its entity) unless the callback returned true to keep it or false to cancel the use.
    PopulateSpawnMenu()client
    Adds the items tab to the spawn menu.
    PostCreateCharacter(owner, char, char_data)server
    Calls the 'AddDefaultItems' plugin hook for a character that has just been created.
    PreItemSave(item_obj, save_table)server
    Adds the position of the item in its inventory, its rotation and, for containers, the instance ids of the contained items to the saved fields.
    PreItemTransfer(item_obj, new_inventory, old_inventory)server
    Calls the on_transfer callback of the item before it changes its inventory.
    SaveCharacterData(owner, char)server
    Stores the instance ids of the player's items in the character, as a comma-separated string.
