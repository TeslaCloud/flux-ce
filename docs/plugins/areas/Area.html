    Methods

    AddAreaToolModes(mode_list)
    Adds the built-in 'Text Area' mode to the area tool.
    HUDPaint()
    Draws the texts of the text areas the local player has recently entered, fading each of them out before it expires.
    LoadData()
    Loads the saved areas from the plugin data storage.
    OneSecond()
    Checks every player who has moved against every area polygon. Runs the area type's callback and notifies the player's client whenever a player enters or leaves an area.
    OnSchemaLoaded()
    Calls the AddAreaToolModes hook so that plugins can add their modes to the area tool.
    PlayerInitialized(actor)
    Sends all stored areas to the player who has just initialized.
    PostDrawOpaqueRenderables(draw_depth, draw_skybox)
    Draws wireframes of the areas that belong to the selected mode of the area tool, and the outline of the area being created, while the local player holds that tool.
    RegisterPermissions()
    Registers the 'areas' level design permission.
    SaveData()
    Saves all stored areas to the plugin data storage.
