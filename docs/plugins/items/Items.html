    Hooks

    CanPlayerDropItem(actor, item_obj)server
    Lets the on_drop callback of the item decide whether the player is able to drop it.
    ClientIncludedSchema(actor)server
    Sends the items lying in the world to the player that has finished loading.
    HUDPaint()client
    Fills the circular progress indicator while the local player holds the use key on an item.
    InitPostEntity()server
    Loads the saved items once the map entities have been created.
    OnEntityCreated(entity)server
    Runs the 'PlayerThrewGrenade' hook on the next tick after a frag grenade is created.
    OnItemCreated(item_obj)server
    Calls the on_created callback of an item instance that has just been created.
    OnItemDataReceived()client
    Requests the item data of every item entity from the server.
    OnPluginLoaded()shared
    Registers the 'items/bases' and 'items' plugin folders and includes the item bases and the items that come with this plugin.
    PlayerCanUseItem(actor, item_obj, action, ...)server
    Prevents menu actions on items that the player does not have, and on items in the world that are too far away from the player, obstructed or not being looked at.
    PlayerUsedItem(actor, item_obj, act, ...)server
    Sends an item that is lying in the world to all clients again after a menu action has been performed on it.
    PlayerUseItemEntity(activator, entity, item_obj)server
    Tells the client of the player to open the menu of the item entity they have used.
    PlayerUseItemMenu(instance_id, is_entity)client
    Opens the menu of an item with its custom buttons and the use, take and drop options.
    PluginIncludeFolder(extra, folder)shared
    Includes the items of a plugin when its 'items' folder is being included.
    PostPlayerSpawn(actor)server
    Calls the on_loadout callback of every item that the player has, on the next tick after they spawn with their character loaded.
    PreDrawHalos()client
    Draws a halo around the item entity that the local player holds the use key on.
    PreSaveCharacter(owner, index)server
    Calls the on_save callback of every item that the player has before their character is saved.
    RegisterConditions()shared
    Registers the 'has_item' and 'has_item_data' conditions.
    SaveData()server
    Saves the item instances and the item entities.
    Think()client
    Aborts taking an item once it gets too far from the center of the local player's screen.
