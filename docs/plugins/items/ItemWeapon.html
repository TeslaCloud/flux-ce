    ItemWeapon class

    Extends ItemEquipable

    Part of items

    Summary

    Methods

    on_save(owner)
    Called on the server before the character of the player that has the item is saved. Stores the contents of the weapon's clips in the item's data.
    on_unload(owner)
    Called on the server when a player presses the unload button in the item's menu. Empties the clips of the weapon and gives the ammo back to the player.
    post_equipped(owner)
    Called when the item gets equipped. Gives the player the weapon, selects it and loads its clips with the ammo that is stored in the item's data.
    post_unequipped(owner)
    Called when the item gets unequipped. Strips the weapon from the player and stores the contents of its clips in the item's data.
