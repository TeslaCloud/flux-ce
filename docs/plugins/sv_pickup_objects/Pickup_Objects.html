    Summary

    Methods

    KeyRelease(actor, key)
    Picks up or drops an object when secondary attack is released while holding fists, and drops the held object when reload is released.
    pickup_at_trace(actor)
    Drops the object the player is holding, or else picks up the entity they are looking at. The entity must be within 2 meters and not held by anyone. The PlayerPickupObject and PlayerDropObject hooks can veto the pickup and the drop.
    PlayerDropObject(actor, ent)
    Restores the dropped object's collision group and starts the player's one second pickup cooldown.
    PlayerPickupObject(actor, ent)
    Denies pickups during the player's one second cooldown and of objects with a mass over 25. Otherwise changes the object's collision group and starts the cooldown.
