    OnActiveCharacterSet(owner, char)
    Restarts the expiry timers of the character's boosts and multipliers, destroys the ones that have already expired and networks the attributes to the player.
    OnCharacterChange(owner, new_char, old_char)
    Removes the attribute timers of the character the player is switching away from.
    PlayerDisconnected(actor)
    Removes the attribute timers of the disconnecting player's character.
    PluginIncludeFolder(extra, folder)
    Includes a plugin's attributes folder when the 'attributes' extra is being loaded.
    PostCreateCharacter(owner, char, char_data)
    Adds an Attribute record for every registered attribute to a new character, using the level chosen during creation or the attribute's minimum.
