    Hooks

    CanContainMoney(object)server
    Allows players that are not bots to hold money.
    CanGiveMoney(actor, target, amount, currency)server
    Checks that the giver may transfer the money and that the target is valid, able to contain money and within 120 units of the giver's eyes.
    CanPlayerDropMoney(actor, amount, currency, pos, trace)server
    Checks that the player may transfer the money, that the drop position is within 120 units of their eyes and that they are not on pickup cooldown.
    CanPlayerPickupMoney(actor, entity)server
    Blocks money pickup while the player or the money entity is on its pickup cooldown.
    CanPlayerTransferMoney(actor, amount, currency)server
    Checks that the amount is positive, the currency exists and the player can afford it.
    CreatePlayerInteractions(menu, target)client
    Adds a money submenu to the player interaction menu with a 'give' option for every currency that is not hidden or that the local player owns.
    OnActiveCharacterSet(owner, character)server
    Networks the currency balances of the newly active character to the player entity.
    OnContainerOpened(panel, inventory_id)client
    Creates or refreshes the money panel below an opened container's inventory.
    OnInventoryRebuild(panel)client
    Creates or refreshes the money panel that sits next to the 'pockets' inventory panel.
    PlayerPickupMoney(actor, entity)server
    Gives the contents of a money entity to the player, notifies them and starts their pickup cooldown.
    PostCreateCharacter(owner, char, char_data)server
    Adds a Currency record with a zero balance for every registered currency to a new character.
    PreContainerOpen(entity)server
    Gives a container zero balances for every currency if it has none yet and networks its balances.

    Currencies:register_currency(id, data)

    shared plugins/currencies/plugin/sh_plugin.lua:21

    Registers a currency. Characters created afterward get a balance record for it.

    Currencies:register_currency('tokens', {
  name = 'currency.tokens.name',
  symbol = 'T',
  decimals = 0,
  hidden = false,
  -- Model of dropped money, and optionally models for larger amounts.
  model = 'models/props_lab/box01a.mdl',
  model_table = { [500] = 'models/props_c17/briefcase001a.mdl' }
})

    Parameters

    • id String currency ID; use lower case, lookups lower-case the ID they are given
    • data Map currency definition: name, symbol, decimals, hidden, model, model_table
