    Summary

    Methods

    is_weapon_raised()
    Checks whether the player's active weapon is raised. Without a valid weapon this is false; physgun, gravity gun, tool gun and camera are always raised. Otherwise the ShouldWeaponBeRaised hook decides, falling back to the networked state.
    set_weapon_raised(raised)
    Raises or lowers the player's active weapon and runs the OnWeaponRaised hook. Does nothing clientside. The CanPlayerRaiseWeapon hook can veto the change.
    toggle_weapon_raised()
    Raises the player's active weapon if it is lowered and lowers it if it is raised. Does nothing clientside.
