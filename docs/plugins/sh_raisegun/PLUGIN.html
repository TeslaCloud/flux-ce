    Implements hook UpdateWeaponRaised

    Lets the weapon fire again (raised, or a weapon that cannot be lowered) or blocks its fire for 60 seconds (lowered). Then calls the weapon's OnRaised or OnLowered method and runs the WeaponRaised or WeaponLowered hook.

    Parameters

    • actor Player
    • weapon Weapon
    • raised Boolean whether the weapon is now raised
    • cur_time Number CurTime() of the change
