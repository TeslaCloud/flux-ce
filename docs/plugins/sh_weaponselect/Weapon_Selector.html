    Plugin hooks and methods

    Part of Weapon Selector

    Summary

    Methods

    HUDPaint()
    Draws the weapon selector while it is open and animates its entries scrolling toward the highlighted weapon.
    HUDShouldDraw(element)
    Hides the default weapon selection HUD.
    make_display(index, tab)
    Builds the five selector entries (two above and two below) around a weapon index. Each entry is a table with the fields weapon, scale, x, y and highlight.
    OnWeaponIndexChange(old_index, index)
    Opens the weapon selector and starts scrolling it from the old index to the new one.
    OnWeaponSelected(index)
    Closes the weapon selector and clears its entries once a weapon has been selected.
    PlayerBindPress(client, bind, pressed)
    Handles the weapon selection binds: invprev, invnext and slot binds move the highlight, attack selects the highlighted weapon while the selector is open. Ignored in vehicles, while attack is held and when ShouldOpenWepselect returns false.
    Think()
    Fades the weapon selector in while it is in use, and fades it out and closes it after five seconds without input.
