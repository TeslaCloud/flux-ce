    Returns the gender of the player's character as a string.

    is_character_loaded()

    save_character()
    Saves the player's active character, if they have one.
    set_active_character(id)
    Makes one of the player's characters their active one. Runs OnCharacterChange if another character was active, networks the basic character data and runs OnActiveCharacterSet.

    Sets a field on the player's active character and networks it under the same name.
