    Player:create_ragdoll_entity(decay, fallen)

    server plugins/ragdoll/plugin/sv_plugin.lua:39

    Spawns a ragdoll copy of the player and stores it as their ragdoll entity. Does nothing if they already have a valid one. A fallen player is frozen, hidden and stripped of their weapons; once the ragdoll is removed they are moved to it and get them back. Serverside only.

    Parameters

    • decay Number optional, defaults to nil seconds the ragdoll remains after the ragdoll entity is reset; nil removes it right away
    • fallen Boolean optional, defaults to false whether the player has fallen over rather than died

    Player:set_ragdoll_state(state)

    server plugins/ragdoll/plugin/sv_plugin.lua:140

    Sets the player's ragdoll state and creates or removes their ragdoll to match it. RAGDOLL_FALLENOVER also starts the 'fallen' action. Serverside only.

    target:set_ragdoll_state(RAGDOLL_FALLENOVER) -- fall over
target:set_ragdoll_state(RAGDOLL_NONE) -- get back up

    Parameters

    • state Number optional, defaults to RAGDOLL_NONE one of the RAGDOLL_ enums
