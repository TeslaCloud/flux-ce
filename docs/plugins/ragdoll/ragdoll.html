    HUDPaint()
    Darkens the screen while the local player is fallen over or getting up, and draws the 'press jump' prompt or the get up progress bar.
    PlayerBindPress(client, bind, pressed)
    Sends the getup command when the player presses jump while fallen over.
    PlayerDeath(victim)
    Cancels the player's current action and puts them into the RAGDOLL_DUMMY state, which leaves a corpse ragdoll.
    PlayerSetupDataTables(target)
    Registers the RagdollState and RagdollEntity data table variables on the player.
    PlayerSpawn(actor)
    Resets the player's ragdoll state to RAGDOLL_NONE when they spawn.
    PlayerThink(actor)
    Runs the PlayerDeathThink hook for dead players that are ragdolled.
